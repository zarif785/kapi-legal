Support

Something wrong? Email zarif.arefein@gmail.com. There is one person on the other end, so please include your iPhone model and iOS version. The Version row in Kapi’s Settings copies exactly what we need; tap it and paste.

A reading or meaning looks wrong

Every reading, meaning, word and sentence in Kapi comes from public dictionary data (KANJIDIC2, JMdict, Tatoeba). Nothing is written by hand. If something looks off, use Report a mistake in Settings — it opens an email with the character filled in.

Subscriptions

  • Cancel: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Kapi. Or Manage subscription inside Kapi. You keep Pro until the paid period ends.
  • Refunds: Apple handles them, not us: support.apple.com/HT204084.
  • New phone: sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore purchases in Settings.
  • Free trial: the yearly plan gives eligible users 3 days free. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid the charge.

Widgets

Long-press the Lock Screen or Home Screen, tap the add button, search for Kapi. The widget shows one kanji at a time and changes on the schedule you pick in Settings. If a widget shows “Open Kapi”, open the app once and it fills in.

Deleting your data

Everything Kapi knows is on your phone. Settings › Reset all progress erases it; deleting the app does too. The only thing outside the phone is an anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat — email us and we delete it within 30 days. Details in the privacy policy.
