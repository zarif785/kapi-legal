Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated: 7 September 2026

In short: Your study progress stays on your phone. The only thing that leaves it is an anonymous purchase record, so Apple and RevenueCat can confirm you paid. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

1. Who we are

Kapi is made by Zarif Raiyan Arefeen, a sole trader, based in New South Wales, Australia. For privacy law we are the "controller", the one responsible for how your data is handled.

Contact: zarif.arefein@gmail.com

This policy covers the Kapi iOS app, its widgets, our website, and our social-media accounts. Every version of this policy is kept; the version number at the top tells you which one you read.

2. What stays on your phone

All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's own private space. We never receive it, and we have no server that could.

  • Study progress. Which kanji you know, the review schedule, check results, the activity feed, the sentence of the day. Kept until you tap "Reset all progress" or delete the app.
  • Settings. Level, daily goal, romaji, voice, appearance, widget hours, reminder switches. Same.
  • Reminders. If you switch a reminder on, iOS asks your permission and the reminder is scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing is sent anywhere. Off by default.
  • A copy of your purchase status, so the widgets know whether Kapi Pro is active even offline.

Speech is generated on your phone by iOS. The reading is never sent to a server.

3. What leaves your phone, and why

  • An anonymous purchase record. When Kapi starts, the RevenueCat software creates a random ID for your copy of the app. When you buy, that ID, your App Store receipt, your plan, and whether you are on a trial go to RevenueCat's servers. Purpose: confirm the purchase, unlock Kapi Pro, restore it on a new phone. Legal basis: needed to deliver what you bought (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), contract), and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud (Art. 6(1)(f)). Kept while your purchase record exists; deleted on request (section 7).
  • A support email, if you send one. "Report a mistake" opens an email you write and send yourself. It carries the app version, iOS version, your level and the character you are reporting. Purpose: fix the mistake. Legal basis: your choice to send it. Kept in our mailbox until the report is closed, at most 12 months.
  • Records the law makes us keep. Apple keeps the tax and payment records for your purchase, not us. If a law requires us to keep something, we keep only that and only for as long as it requires (Art. 6(1)(c)).

The anonymous ID is a random string. It is not your name, email, Apple ID or device serial number, and we cannot work out who you are from it.

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data, or advertising identifier. There are no accounts and no passwords.

4. Who else handles data, and where

  • Apple Inc. Your App Store purchase, billing and refunds. Apple runs the App Store and processes the payment under its own policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States). The anonymous ID, the receipt, your plan and trial status. RevenueCat confirms purchases for us and works under our instructions as a "processor". Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Nobody else. No analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting, no tracking. If we ever add a service that receives data from the app, we update this policy and the version number first, and the App Store privacy label with it.

5. Social media and advertising

We post about Kapi and run ads on TikTok, Instagram and other platforms. Two things to know:

  • On those platforms, the platform collects the data, not us. What you see, tap or comment on there is governed by that platform's privacy policy. We see only what the platform shows every advertiser: totals, never individuals.
  • Nothing from those platforms is inside the app. Kapi contains no TikTok, Meta or other advertising software, so an ad you tapped cannot be linked to what you do in Kapi. If that ever changes, iOS will ask you first through the "Allow tracking" prompt, and this policy will say so before it happens.

If you message us on a platform, we use your message to reply and for nothing else.

6. Data leaving the EU or UK

The purchase record goes to RevenueCat's servers in the United States. That transfer is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in RevenueCat's Data Processing Agreement (https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa).

7. Your rights, and how to use them

If you are in the EU, UK or a place with similar law, you can:

  • See what we hold about you.
  • Correct it.
  • Delete it.
  • Limit or object to how we use it.
  • Take a copy in a usable format.
  • Withdraw a permission you gave (for example, switch reminders off).
  • Complain to your data protection authority: in Australia, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (https://www.oaic.gov.au); in the EU or UK, your national authority.

If you are in California, you can also ask what we collect, ask us to delete or correct it, and you will never be treated worse for asking. We do not sell personal information for money and we do not share it for cross-context advertising. There is nothing to opt out of.

How to use these rights:

  • On your phone. Most of your data is already in your hands. Settings → Reset all progress erases every piece of study data and every setting; deleting the app erases everything. Widgets clear on their next refresh.
  • At RevenueCat. Email zarif.arefein@gmail.com and paste the details the Version row in Settings copies (they include the anonymous purchase ID). We delete the customer record within 30 days. Your Apple purchase itself lives with Apple and stays in your Apple account history; that is how "Restore purchases" works.

We answer within 30 days.

8. Children

Kapi is for adult learners and is not directed at children. We do not ask for your age and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). Because the app keeps no personal data, there is nothing for a child to give us. If a parent tells us a child has used Kapi and something reached us by email, we delete it. Contact: zarif.arefein@gmail.com.

9. Tracking

Kapi does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not show the "Allow tracking" prompt, because there is nothing to allow.

10. Security, in plain words

Your progress is stored in the app's own private space on your phone and protected by whatever locks your phone. The purchase record travels to RevenueCat over an encrypted connection and RevenueCat's reply is signed, so the app can tell a real one from a fake. We keep no server of our own, so there is no database of users to breach. If RevenueCat ever reported a breach affecting you, we would tell you at the address above and through an app update, within the time the law requires.

11. Changes

When this policy changes we update the version and the date at the top and, for anything that matters to you, say so inside the app before it applies. Old versions are available on request.
